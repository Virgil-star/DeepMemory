# HEIR ÆTERNA

A speculative-fiction trilogy. Development material.

*Heir Æterna* — the eternal heir, and the heir to eternity. Iris Rask inherits a
world she never agreed to, and over three books she stops being its instrument and
becomes its author.

> The inventor believed he could save reality by creating someone who would never
> permit it to change. Instead he created the first person with the standing and
> the courage to demand that reality justify itself.

| | Book | Question | She becomes |
|---|---|---|---|

| **II** | **The Instrument** | *What was I made for?* | an **executor** |

## Reading order

Start with `trilogy.md` for the shape, then `bible.md` for the rules. Read
`craft-research.md` to see which structural decisions were made on purpose and
why. `ch01-the-missing-day.md` is the voice.

## Open decisions

- **Book titles** — *The Inheritance* / *The Instrument* / *The Appeal* is a
  proposal, applied so the rest of the material is consistent. Reverting is a
  one-line change in each file.
- **Chapter counts** — 38 / 36 / 34 is a shape, not a commitment.
- **Thea's death** (Book Two ch. 23) is recommended rather than settled. The
  alternative — alive and unreachable — is weaker: it keeps rescue on the table,
  which the cost ladder needs removed.
- **The father's name.** Edmund Rask is placeholder-strength. Iris is the only name
  carried over from the treatment and the only one that should be considered fixed.
