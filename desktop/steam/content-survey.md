# Content survey: draft answers

Steam requires a content survey before it will review the store page or the
build. It has three parts. You answer it and you are responsible for the
answers; Valve compares them with what is in the build. These drafts describe
what is actually in the game.

## 1. General content

This part produces the regional age ratings. Answer from the facts below.

The game is text and interface only. It has no images of people, no depicted
violence, no blood, no nudity, no sexual content, no gambling, no drug or
alcohol use, no profanity, no online interaction and no purchases.

## 2. Mature content

Nothing is depicted. The game does refer, in short written passages, to:

- war, raids and lost battles
- famine and plague, including a plague that "takes the young first"
- public punishment, and a square "washed by morning"
- coups, and a ruler pulled from a palace by a crowd
- a nuclear exchange, as one of the ways the game can end
- a ruler whose mind breaks under the voice

Suggested description, if the survey asks for one:

> Text-only references to war, famine, plague, public punishment and nuclear
> war. Nothing is depicted.

## 3. Generative AI

Valve asks about AI used "in creating content that ships with your game, and is
consumed by players", and names artwork, sound, narrative and localization. It
says plainly that AI tools used only to work faster are not what this part is
about.

**Pre-generated content: yes. This must be disclosed.** Draft, to correct
against what you actually used:

> The opening film and the key art were created with AI image and video
> generation tools. The game's written text, including its events and endings,
> was drafted with AI assistance and edited by the developer. Music and sound
> effects are synthesised at run time by the game's own code, not generated by
> an AI model.

By declaring it you are also confirming the usual promise: nothing in it is
illegal or infringing, and the game matches its marketing.

**Live-generated content: no.** The game runs no AI model. Its text is fixed
and its events are chosen by ordinary seeded randomness, so no guardrails
answer is needed. That the story is about an artificial mind does not change
this.

Name the tools if the form asks. Only you know which ones made the film and
the art.

## Other declarations you will be asked for

- **Rights.** That you hold the rights to everything in the build. The icons
  inside the game and on the achievements are Lucide (ISC licence). The fonts
  are Montserrat, Source Serif 4 and Source Sans 3 (SIL Open Font Licence).
  Electron and its components carry their own notices, which ship in the build
  as `LICENSE.electron.txt` and `LICENSES.chromium.html`.
- **Third-party accounts, DRM, anti-cheat, online play, in-game purchases:**
  none.
- **Data collection:** none. The game makes no network requests of its own.
