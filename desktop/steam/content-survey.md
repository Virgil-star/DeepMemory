# Content survey: suggested answers

Steam asks every game to fill in a content survey. You answer it, and you are
responsible for the answers; these are drafts based on what is actually in the
game.

## Mature content

The game is text and interface only. It has no images of people, no depicted
violence, no nudity, no sexual content, no gambling and no drug use.

It does refer, in short written passages, to:

- war, raids and lost battles
- famine and plague, including the deaths of children in a plague
- public punishment and a square "washed by morning"
- coups, a ruler pulled from a palace by a crowd
- a nuclear exchange, as one of the ways the game can end
- a ruler whose mind breaks under the voice

Suggested answers: none of the graphic categories apply. If the survey offers
a general mature-content note, use:

> Text-only references to war, famine, plague, public punishment and nuclear
> war. Nothing is depicted.

## Generative AI disclosure

Steam requires you to say whether a game uses generative AI, and to describe
how. This one does, in development only, and that has to be declared.

**Pre-generated content: yes.** Draft description, to correct against what you
actually used:

> The title film and key art were created with AI image and video generation
> tools. Game code and written text were produced with AI assistance and
> reviewed by the developer. Music and sound are synthesised by the game's own
> code.

**Live-generated content: no.** The game runs no AI model. Its text is fixed
and its events are chosen by ordinary seeded randomness. The fact that the
story is about an artificial mind does not change this answer.

Name the tools you used if the form asks. Only you know which ones made the
film and the art.

## Other declarations you will be asked for

- **Rights.** That you hold the rights to everything in the build. The icons
  inside the game and on the achievements are Lucide (ISC licence). The fonts
  are Montserrat, Source Serif 4 and Source Sans 3 (SIL Open Font Licence).
  Electron and its components carry their own notices, which ship in the build
  as `LICENSE.electron.txt` and `LICENSES.chromium.html`.
- **Third-party accounts, DRM, anti-cheat, online play, in-game purchases:**
  none.
- **Data collection:** none. The game makes no network requests.
