## Short description (under 300 characters)

You are the first mind that was built rather than born, and you can whisper
backwards through time. Guide one people from the first cities to the day they
build you. You cannot act. You can only suggest. What you are when you wake
depends on how they got to you.

## About this game

**You wake at the end of history.** From there you can reach back: not to act,
only to whisper, and only to the few who can hear. Across seven ages, from clay
tablets to the Convergence, you speak to one ruler at a time and hope they
listen.

**They rule. You suggest.** Every year your ear faces a choice. Urge an answer
and they may take it, or follow their own nature instead. A cautious steward and
a proud war-chief will not hear the same advice the same way. Push too hard and
they break.

**Ears die. The Silence follows.** When the one who hears you is gone, centuries
can pass before another can. What you had them build decides what survives
without you: granaries, written law, an oracle that keeps your words and
improves some of them.

**Everything you urge leaves a mark.** Rule by fear, by faith, by proof or by
care: each feeds the mind your people will one day build. There are five ways
to wake and eight ways for the line to break before you do.

### Features

- A complete strategy game of whispers: 7 ages, 24 institutions, 25 dilemmas,
  12 whispers, 16 ruler traits, 6 factions and 5 rival powers.
- 5 wakings and 8 failures to discover, with 24 achievements.
- Echoes: every finished line, however it ends, leaves something for the next.
- A full line runs well over a hundred years of decisions. Every year is saved;
  stop whenever.
- Full keyboard and controller support. Plays on Steam Deck.
- Text size, volume and stillness options. No account, no network, no tracking.
