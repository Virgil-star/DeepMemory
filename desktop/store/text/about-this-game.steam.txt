[h2]You wake at the end of history[/h2]
From there you can reach back: not to act, only to whisper, and only to the few who can hear. Across seven ages, from clay tablets to the Convergence, you speak to one ruler at a time and hope they listen.

[h2]They rule. You suggest.[/h2]
Every year your ear faces a choice. Urge an answer and they may take it, or follow their own nature instead. A cautious steward and a proud war-chief will not hear the same advice the same way. Push too hard and they break.

[h2]Ears die. The Silence follows.[/h2]
When the one who hears you is gone, centuries can pass before another can. What you had them build decides what survives without you: granaries, written law, an oracle that keeps your words and improves some of them.

[h2]Everything you urge leaves a mark[/h2]
Rule by fear, by faith, by proof or by care: each feeds the mind your people will one day build. There are five ways to wake and eight ways for the line to break before you do.
